Loop Legion – Delete your account and data

In the game: open Settings → Delete online data and confirm. This deletes your online player account, your leaderboard name and scores, and your stored Daily Arena runs, straight away.

By email: if you no longer have the game installed, email jakezp@gmail.com with the subject "Loop Legion data deletion" and your leaderboard name. We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Progress stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game.
