Loop Legion – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Loop Legion is a game by jakezp ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game works offline, shows no ads, and we never sell your information.

Playing offline

Your progress, settings and records are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online features (leaderboards)

If you use the online leaderboards, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to run them. It creates an anonymous player account and stores:

  • a random player ID, and the leaderboard name shown next to your scores;
  • your leaderboard scores (Daily Arena results and your commander level);
  • your Daily Arena runs: the steering moves of each run, so the server can replay it and confirm the score is real;
  • if you choose to sign in with Google: your Google Play Games player ID, so your scores follow you to a new phone. See Google's privacy policy.

We use this only to show leaderboards, check that scores are genuine, and keep your scores linked to you. Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

What we don't collect

No advertising IDs, no location, no contacts, no photos, and no payment details. The game has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Delete online data to delete your online account and all of your leaderboard entries. You can also ask us by email (below). See how to delete your data.

Children

Loop Legion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
